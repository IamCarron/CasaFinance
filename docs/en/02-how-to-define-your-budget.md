# 📊 02. How to Define Your Household Budget

The goal of budgeting in CasaFinance is to determine with exact precision **how much money needs to be transferred into the joint account on the 1st of every month** so that all bills and daily living expenses are fully covered.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Structure of a Couple's Budget

A balanced household budget consists of three distinct categories:

### A) Fixed Recurring Bills (Exact Invoices)
Known expenses that are typically charged automatically via direct debit:
- **Rent or Mortgage:** e.g., `$850` *(Housing)*
- **HOA / Building Maintenance:** e.g., `$60` *(Housing)*
- **Home Internet & Fiber WiFi:** e.g., `$35` *(Internet)*
- **Shared Subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify Duo...):** e.g., `$25` *(Subscriptions)*
- **Home Insurance:** e.g., `$20` *(monthly average)*

### B) Variable Budget Envelopes (Estimated Consumption)
Living costs that fluctuate each month but are paid using the joint account debit card:
- **Groceries & Food:** e.g., `$400` *(Weekly food shopping and household items)*
- **Utilities (Electricity, Water, Gas):** e.g., `$130` *(Monthly estimated average)*
- **Couple Dining & Entertainment:** e.g., `$150` *(Monthly envelope for dinners and dates)*

### C) Household Emergency Reserve Buffer
- **House Emergency Fund:** e.g., `$100` *(Buffer to build a cash cushion for appliance repairs, unexpected bills, or holidays)*.

---

## 🔢 2. Automatic Bank Transfer Allocations

Once you enter your budget line items (e.g., total sum = **$1,770**), the app displays the exact breakdown:

| Partner | Base Income | Split % | Transfer to Joint Account |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Alex** | $2,100 | 60% | **$1,062.00** |
| **Sam** | $1,400 | 40% | **$708.00** |
| **Total Household** | **$3,500** | **100%** | **$1,770.00** |

On the 1st of every month, Alex sets up a standing order of $1,062 and Sam sets up $708 to the joint account.

---

## 📈 3. Real-Time Tracking on the Dashboard

Throughout the month, whenever you buy groceries or a utility bill is charged:
1. The transaction is logged in the **Expense Ledger**.
2. That amount is deducted from the remaining joint account balance.
3. The Dashboard progress bar alerts you if your spending pace is on track or exceeding the monthly budget.

---

## 💡 4. Practical Tips

- **Round up utility bills:** For electricity and heating, calculate based on colder winter months to avoid shortfalls.
- **Keep personal indulgences out of the joint budget:** Individual clothing, solo hobbies, or tech gadgets should be paid from your **personal private account**.
- **Bonus months:** If one partner receives a quarterly bonus or extra paycheck, click **"Adjust Month Income"** in the app to rebalance the split for that month only.
