# 🧠 01. Financial Philosophy & Methodology

Managing shared finances is one of the leading causes of stress in relationships. Most financial disagreements do not arise from a lack of money, but from **a lack of clear, equitable ground rules**.

**CasaFinance** is built upon the gold standard of modern personal finance for couples: **The 3-Account Model**.

---

## 🏦 1. The 3-Account Model

Instead of merging all income into a single joint account (which often triggers friction over individual personal purchases) or maintaining a tedious manual spreadsheet of small debts, this model establishes three distinct financial compartments:

```
                  ┌────────────────────────┐
                  │ 💑 COUPLE FINANCES     │
                  └───────────┬────────────┘
                              │
         ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
         │                                         │
         ▼                                         ▼
┌─────────────────┐                       ┌─────────────────┐
│ 👤 YOUR ACCOUNT │                       │ 👤 PARTNER'S    │
│    (PERSONAL)   │                       │    ACCOUNT      │
│ (Your salary)   │                       │ (Their salary)  │
└────────┬────────┘                       └────────┬────────┘
         │                                         │
         │ (Equitable share)     (Equitable share)
         │       e.g. 60%                 e.g. 40% │
         └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                              ▼
                  ┌───────────────────────┐
                  │ 🏠 JOINT HOUSE ACCOUNT│
                  │  - Rent / Mortgage    │
                  │  - Groceries & Food   │
                  │  - Utilities & WiFi   │
                  │  - Savings Pot        │
                  └───────────────────────┘
```

1. **Your Personal Account:** You receive your paycheck. After transferring your fair share to the household on day 1, the remaining balance is **100% yours** for personal hobbies, clothing, gifts, or solo outings without asking for permission.
2. **Your Partner's Personal Account:** They receive their paycheck, transfer their fair share, and maintain total financial autonomy.
3. **The Joint House Account:** Used **strictly for shared living expenses, groceries, utilities, and joint activities**.

## ⚖️ 2. Proportional Splitting vs 50/50

### ❌ The Problem with Strict 50/50 Splitting
Consider a couple where:
- Partner A earns **$2,500/month**.
- Partner B earns **$1,200/month**.
- Total household shared expenses equal **$1,600/month**.

If they split **50/50** ($800 each):
- For Partner A, $800 represents only **32%** of their salary (leaving $1,700 for personal savings and lifestyle).
- For Partner B, $800 represents a heavy **66%** of their salary (leaving only $400 for the entire month).

This causes financial strain, lifestyle disparity, and underlying tension.

### ✅ The Proportional Solution in CasaFinance
CasaFinance computes the exact financial effort ratio:
- **Total household net income:** $2,500 + $1,200 = $3,700
- **Partner A ratio:** `$2,500 / $3,700 = 67.5%` -> Transfers `$1,080`
- **Partner B ratio:** `$1,200 / $3,700 = 32.5%` -> Transfers `$520`

Both partners contribute exactly **43.2% of their respective income**. The financial sacrifice is 100% fair and balanced.

---

## 🔀 3. One-Off Exceptions (Forced 50/50 Override)

While proportional contribution is the default recommendation, couples occasionally prefer a 50/50 split for specific items:
- **Mortgage principal payments** (when the home deed is owned 50/50).
- **Special shared indulgences** (a luxury holiday or designer furniture).

CasaFinance allows you to toggle **"Force 50/50"** on specific budget line items or individual bot expense entries (`"80 Dinner 50/50"`).
